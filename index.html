About Me

I am a final-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Derek Hoiem.

I aim to build intelligent agents that plan under partial information, remember what matters, and draw on past experience to avoid repeating failures. More specifically:

  • Closed-loop Planning: How can we make decisions under partial information? Each decision must build on past observations while shaping what comes next.

  • Memory Representation: How can a model compress its past into a compact, updatable state that preserves essential details and turns experience into reusable lessons?

  • Experience Retrieval: How can an agent retrieve useful past experiences or distilled reflective lessons?

I'm seeking full-time industry roles starting in summer 2027. Please email me if you know of a good match!
